Privacy Policy

The North Watch (Gasper Jager s.p., Slovenia) — last updated 23 August 2026

This policy explains what data our games collect and why. It applies to Space Tanks Mayhem, Snake Arena, and other games we publish under The North Watch.

The short version

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or location. Our games are supported by advertising and optional in-app purchases, and those services collect some data as described below.

Data collected by us

Nothing that identifies you personally. Game progress and whether you have purchased "Remove Ads" are stored locally on your own device, not on our servers. We do not operate servers that store player data.

Data collected by third parties

  • Google AdMob — serves the ads in our games and may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate (coarse) location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data in order to show and measure ads. See Google's policy.
  • GameAnalytics — collects anonymous gameplay statistics, such as how far you got in a run and when a session started, so we can improve the game. See GameAnalytics' policy.
  • Apple Game Center (Snake Arena only) — if you are signed in to Game Center, your score is submitted to Apple's leaderboard under your Game Center nickname. Signing in is optional; decline it and the game hides the leaderboard and submits nothing. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — process any purchases you make. We never see or store your payment details.

Local multiplayer (Snake Arena)

Snake Arena can host or join a game over your local Wi-Fi network. While you are using that feature the game advertises itself on the local network and exchanges gameplay data — ship positions, scores, and the device's local IP address — directly with the other players' devices. None of it passes through, or is stored on, any server of ours, and nothing is sent over the internet. It stops as soon as you leave the multiplayer game.

Advertising choices

On iOS, the game asks for your permission before any tracking takes place (App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads. Buying "Remove Ads" stops ads entirely.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights (GDPR)

As we hold no personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to export or erase. For data held by the third parties above, please contact them directly using the links provided. You may also contact us with any question about this policy.

Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
