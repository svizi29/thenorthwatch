What it does

One chat, a whole crew

Tell the Boss what you want. It splits the job into small tasks and hands them out.

Tests decide

The Boss writes the tests first. A task is done when they pass, not when a model says so.

Cheap first

Low-cost models do the building. A task that keeps failing moves up to a stronger model, and Swarm asks before it uses the expensive one.

Every cent shown

Each model call is logged with its real cost, next to what the same job would cost on one big model.

Your code stays put

On an existing project the crew works on a copy and a separate branch. Swarm never merges or pushes.

Reports, not walls of text

Each finished job comes back as a short report: what was asked, what was done, what it cost.

Works with Claude

Claude Desktop and Claude Code can hand work to the crew and get a short summary back.

Voice

Dictate a request and hear the answer in a sentence or two.

Bring your own key

Swarm has no account and no key inside. You paste your own OpenRouter key on first launch; it is kept in the macOS Keychain and you pay OpenRouter directly for what the models use.

It needs

  • macOS 13 Ventura or later
  • A Mac with Apple silicon
  • Node.js 24 or later, installed first
  • Your own OpenRouter key

First steps

  • Open Swarm.dmg and drag Swarm to Applications.
  • Open Swarm, go to Settings and paste your OpenRouter key.
  • Type what you want built and press Send.

Everything runs on your Mac. Your requests go only to the models you pick, through your own key. The app is signed and notarized by Apple.
