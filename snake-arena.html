Snake Arena

A snake game with rockets. Every crystal you collect makes your trail longer — and your trail is just as deadly to you as it is to everyone else.

Coming soon to the App Store

Captured in-game on iPhone.

What it is

Classic snake rules, rebuilt in 3D and played at speed.

One thumb, no buttons

Drag anywhere on the screen to steer. That is the whole control scheme. Your rocket never stops moving, so every turn is a commitment.

Your own tail kills you

Every crystal makes the trail behind you longer. Grow far enough and the arena starts to feel small for reasons that are entirely your fault.

Ten rival rockets

Each one drags its own lethal trail across your path, and none of them are looking where you are going.

The arena moves

The safe zone opens up as you survive, pushing you outward into more traffic and more rock. Long runs get more dangerous, not less.

Play together on Wi-Fi

Up to six players in one arena over local Wi-Fi. No internet connection, no accounts, no lobbies — just people in the same room fighting for the same crystals.

Nothing to sign up for

No account, no energy timer, no sync. Open it and play. Your best score lives on your own device.

Offline LAN mode

Six rockets, one room, no server.

One person taps HOST A GAME and everyone else on the same Wi-Fi finds it automatically — or types the host's code if the network is being difficult. There is no server in the middle, which is why it works with the internet switched off entirely. Crash in multiplayer and you respawn in a couple of seconds, so nobody sits out watching.
