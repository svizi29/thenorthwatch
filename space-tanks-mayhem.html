What it is

Arena survival tank combat — three minutes at a time.

Endless waves

Every wave adds another hostile. Scouts flank, heavies soak damage, dual-guns punish you for standing still.

Five battlefields

Lunar, Mars, Ice, Toxic and Alien sectors — each with its own light, terrain and cover.

A real arsenal

Main gun, grenades, a nitro dash and a boost hop. Learn to use all four or you will not see wave five.

Destructible cover

The rocks and wrecks you hide behind come apart under fire. Position is temporary.

One-hand controls

A floating stick under your left thumb, action buttons under your right. No tutorial needed.

No account, no waiting

Open it and play. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to sync, no energy timer.

The setup

Rogue Battalion.

A command AI went rogue and took every tank in the fleet with it. Yours held. The mission is the oldest one there is: survive, reach the data core, and take back one sector at a time. Between waves the AI talks to you. It is not encouraging.
